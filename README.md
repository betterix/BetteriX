## Who I am

I’m BetteriX — I focus on DevOps, infrastructure as code (IaC), automation, and site reliability. I build tools and pipelines that reduce manual work, increase confidence in deployments, and make operating services easier.

- ✅ Practical automation and tooling for real teams
- ✅ Infrastructure-as-code and reproducible environments
- ✅ CI/CD pipelines, observability, and cost-aware ops
- ✅ Security-minded automation and deployments

## What I work on

- Infrastructure provisioning and management (Terraform, CloudFormation)
- Configuration management and automation (Ansible, shell tooling)
- Containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes, Helm)
- CI/CD and GitOps (GitHub Actions, Argo CD, pipelines)
- Observability and alerting (Prometheus, Grafana, Loki)
- SRE practices: SLOs, runbooks, incident automation
